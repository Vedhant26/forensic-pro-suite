### Is your feature request related to a problem? Please describe.
Currently, all authenticated users have similar access levels. In real forensic environments, different roles (Analyst, Lead Investigator, Admin) require different permissions.

### Describe the solution you'd like
Implement Role-Based Access Control:

- Roles:
  - Admin (full access to all cases + settings)
  - Investigator (create + analyze cases)
  - Viewer (read-only access)
- Protect routes like `/dashboard`, `/cases`, `/reports`
- Enforce role checks in both frontend and backend (FastAPI + NextAuth)

### Describe alternatives you've considered
Single-role authentication, but it lacks real-world forensic workflow control.

### Additional context
Improves security, audit compliance, and realistic forensic simulation.
